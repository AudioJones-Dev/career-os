# Opportunity Scoring Model

Score each verified opening out of 100.

## Weighted Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Compensation | 20 | 0 below $90K; strongest score when the published range comfortably exceeds the floor |
| Remote and Florida eligibility | 15 | Full score only when Florida is permitted and remote status is explicit |
| Transferable experience fit | 20 | Match to operations, implementation, customer operations, field service, CRM, systems, or transformation evidence |
| Requirements fit | 15 | Degree, years, domain background, licenses, travel, and technical expectations |
| Portfolio leverage | 10 | Ability to use AJ Digital OS, FR&L, HDIKIT, or Business Memory as credible proof |
| Role trajectory | 10 | Compensation growth, responsibility, marketability, and future options |
| Employer quality | 5 | Stability, product relevance, reputation, and clarity of the operating model |
| Application access | 5 | Freshness, direct application, referral path, recruiter visibility, and applicant competition indicators |

## Confidence Adjustment

Every score must include a confidence label:

- **High:** salary, remote eligibility, requirements, and employer posting are directly verified.
- **Medium:** one material element is inferred or comes from a secondary source.
- **Low:** salary or remote eligibility is unclear; do not treat as application-ready.

## Hard Disqualifiers

- Published maximum base salary below $90,000
- Florida excluded from remote eligibility
- Mandatory license, clearance, or degree with no equivalent-experience clause when clearly enforced
- Role is primarily commission-based or misclassified as remote
- Posting is expired, duplicated, unverifiable, or appears fraudulent
- Responsibilities are materially outside the target career thesis

## Decision Bands

- **85–100:** Priority application; tailor immediately
- **70–84:** Qualified application
- **60–69:** Watchlist or pursue only with a referral
- **Below 60:** Reject

## Evidence Mapping Requirement

Before applying, identify at least three verified proof points from:

- AJ Digital OS V1
- Florida Ramp & Lift FieldOps platform and operational foundation
- HDIKIT
- Business Memory architecture
- Enterprise customer operations experience
- Team leadership, training, escalations, or CRM use
- Google Project Management, UX, or Digital Marketing credentials
