# Remote $90K+ Search Strategy

## Objective

Secure a full-time remote U.S. role with a base salary of at least $90,000 while preserving the option to live in Southwest Florida.

## Primary Role Families

1. Business operations and operational excellence
2. Implementation and professional services
3. Program and project delivery
4. Customer and service operations
5. Business systems and CRM operations
6. Digital transformation and AI operations
7. Knowledge operations and AI governance

## Source Priority

1. Employer career pages
2. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday postings
3. Indeed and LinkedIn
4. Built In, Wellfound, YC Jobs, and Remote-focused boards
5. Recruiter outreach and referrals

## Hard Filters

- Full-time employee role unless an unusually strong contract opportunity is explicitly evaluated
- Remote within the United States
- Florida permitted
- Published base salary reaches at least $90,000, or credible market evidence supports that floor
- Role responsibilities materially align with operations, implementation, systems, service delivery, or transformation
- No required professional license or clearance Tyrone does not hold

## Targeting Allocation

- 40% Business Operations / Operations Manager
- 25% Implementation / Program Management
- 15% Customer or Service Operations
- 10% Business Systems / CRM Operations
- 10% AI Operations / Digital Transformation stretch roles

## Application Threshold

Apply when:

- Opportunity score is 70 or higher, and
- No hard disqualifier exists, and
- At least three verified experience assets map to the role, and
- A tailored application can be produced without inventing credentials or outcomes.

Roles scoring 60–69 enter a watchlist. Roles below 60 are normally rejected.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

- Monday–Friday: source and score new roles
- Daily: apply to the highest-scoring qualified opportunities
- Twice weekly: follow up on prior applications where a contact path exists
- Friday: review response rates, rejection patterns, and keyword gaps
- Monthly: revise positioning only when market evidence justifies it
